*SPY×FAMILY* follows a spy named Twilight whose new mission requires him to assemble a family. His chosen wife, Yor, is secretly an assassin, while his adopted daughter, Anya, can read minds. The humour comes from their concealed identities and the contrast between high-stakes espionage and ordinary family life. Its combination of action, school comedy and warmth makes it an accessible recommendation for anyone looking for something playful and charming to enjoy between busier days.
