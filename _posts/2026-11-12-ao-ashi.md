*Ao Ashi* follows Ashito Aoi, a talented but unpolished teenager from Ehime, as he pursues his dream of becoming a professional footballer. Its appeal lies not only in the excitement of matches, but in its attention to movement, spatial awareness and teamwork. Progress means learning to read the game and contribute to a team, rather than relying on individual talent alone. A rewarding choice for viewers who enjoy football tactics and stories about the patient, sometimes difficult process of improvement.
