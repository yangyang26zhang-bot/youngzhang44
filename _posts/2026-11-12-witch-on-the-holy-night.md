*Witch on the Holy Night* is an upcoming animated film from ufotable, adapted from TYPE-MOON’s story about Aoko Aozaki, a high-school student who is also an apprentice magician. Living with Alice Kuonji, Aoko balances ordinary school life with her magical training until an encounter with Soujuurou Shizuki, a newcomer from the countryside, begins to change their everyday world. With its premise of magic hidden beneath ordinary life, the film is one to look out for if you enjoy contemporary fantasy, coming-of-age stories and unexpected connections between people from very different backgrounds.

As of this entry's publication, the [announced Japanese theatrical release](https://www.aniplex.co.jp/lineup/mahoyo-movie/news/detail/?id=70803) is **20 November 2026**.
