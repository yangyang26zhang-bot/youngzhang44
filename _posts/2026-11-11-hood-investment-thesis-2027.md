## Investment thesis

*Views as of 11 November 2026.*

My investment thesis for HOOD in 2027 combines the appeal of Robinhood's product experience with the growth of prediction markets and a potential recovery in cryptocurrency trading activity. I am interested in how these opportunities could reinforce a broader financial-services business over time.

## Product experience and a compounding business

I particularly like Robinhood's accessible interface and the way its expanding range of products can fit within one platform. My thesis is that a strong user experience can help attract customers, while additional services create opportunities to deepen engagement, retain assets and increase revenue per customer.

This is what I mean by a potentially compounding business model: products may reinforce one another, allowing growth to come from both new customers and broader relationships with existing ones. Robinhood's [Q3 2026 results](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-third-quarter-2026-results) show revenue across options, equities, event contracts, crypto, net interest and other services. Whether this breadth translates into durable earnings growth remains central to my thesis.

## The pullback and valuation

The [decline through October and early November 2026](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hood/history/) has made the stock more interesting to me, but a lower share price does not automatically make it inexpensive.

In the Stock Analysis snapshots reviewed on 11 November 2026, [HOOD](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/hood/statistics/) still traded at a higher trailing P/E than [SoFi](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/sofi/statistics/), at approximately 39 times versus 30 times. A simple trailing-P/E comparison with [Coinbase](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/coin/statistics/) was not meaningful because Coinbase had negative trailing earnings. My case for HOOD therefore depends on the quality and sustainability of future growth, rather than on it being the cheapest stock among these companies.

## Prediction markets

Prediction markets are an especially interesting part of the investment case. Institutional market makers are participating: for example, Robinhood named [Susquehanna as a partner and liquidity provider](https://robinhood.com/newsroom/robinhood-prediction-markets-joint-venture/) for its exchange venture.

I see this participation as a potential support for deeper liquidity and a better trading experience. That could help prediction markets grow and strengthen Robinhood's offering. The key investment question is how effectively this activity converts into lasting revenue and profitability for Robinhood.

## Crypto exposure within a broader business

A recovery in cryptocurrency trading activity could provide another source of upside. I am interested in obtaining some exposure to that opportunity through Robinhood's wider financial-services platform.

When comparing HOOD with COIN, CRCL, MSTR and BMNR, I focus on the different business models and sources of crypto exposure rather than treating the tickers as interchangeable. My preference for HOOD is based on the possibility that its broader revenue mix could make its business performance less dependent on a single crypto-related driver.

For my purposes, this makes HOOD an appealing way to seek crypto exposure alongside other growth opportunities. It is a thesis about business diversification; establishing a lower stock-price beta to crypto would require a defined benchmark, observation period and analysis of returns.

## Questions to follow

- Could prediction markets become a meaningful contributor to the business?
- Are additional products improving customer retention, assets held and revenue per customer?
- What evidence would indicate a sustained recovery in cryptocurrency trading activity?
- How much does revenue diversification reduce dependence on trading volumes and crypto market conditions?
- How much expected growth is already reflected in the valuation?
- What developments would change my view?
