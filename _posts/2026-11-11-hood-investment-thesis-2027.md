## Investment thesis

My investment thesis for HOOD in 2027 rests on two possibilities: the growth of prediction markets and a potential recovery in cryptocurrency trading activity. I am interested in whether these developments could create a meaningful benefit for the business.

Prediction markets represent a potential growth opportunity, while renewed cryptocurrency trading activity could provide another source of upside. The investment case depends on how these expectations translate into business results and how much optimism is already reflected in the share price.

## Questions to follow

- Could prediction markets become a meaningful contributor to the business?
- What evidence would indicate a sustained recovery in cryptocurrency trading activity?
- How sensitive is the thesis to trading volumes and market conditions?
- How much expected growth is already reflected in the valuation?
- What developments would change my view?
