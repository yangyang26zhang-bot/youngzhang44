## Investment thesis

My investment thesis for TER in 2027 centres on demand for semiconductor testing, particularly wafer and high-bandwidth memory (HBM) testing. I am interested in whether rising demand in these areas could translate into stronger business performance and an attractive investment opportunity.

A second part of the thesis is the potential for longer-term upside from robotics. I see this as an additional source of possible growth alongside the semiconductor testing argument.

## Questions to follow

- How directly could increased wafer and HBM testing demand benefit TER?
- What would demonstrate that this demand is translating into sustainable earnings?
- How meaningful could robotics become, and over what time horizon?
- How much of these expectations is already reflected in the share price?
- What evidence would weaken either part of the thesis?
