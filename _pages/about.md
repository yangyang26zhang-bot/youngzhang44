I am Young Zhang, a PhD student in the Department of Mathematics at Imperial College London, where I am fortunate to be supervised by [Philip Ernst](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/p.ernst/about) and [Riccardo Passeggeri](https://profiles.imperial.ac.uk/riccardo.passeggeri).

Before starting my PhD, I completed my BSc in Mathematics at Imperial College London and my MASt in Mathematics (Part III) at the University of Cambridge, where I was a member of St John's College.

My research interests lie in applied probability, with a particular focus on extreme value theory, optimal transport, and stochastic control. I am supported by a President's PhD Scholarship.

## Education

- **Current:** PhD in Statistics, Imperial College London.
- **2024-2025:** MASt in Mathematics (Part III), University of Cambridge, St John's College.
- **2021-2024:** BSc in Mathematics, Imperial College London, First-Class Honours.

## Current Research

I am currently studying extreme value theory for discrete-time series in Wasserstein space, including asymptotic laws and clustering behaviour. This work also explores applications to transformer-layer settings through theoretical analysis and numerical simulations.
