*Jujutsu Kaisen* follows Yuji Itadori, a high-school student drawn into a hidden world where curses emerge from human negativity. Joining a school for jujutsu sorcerers, he confronts supernatural threats alongside a memorable group of classmates and teachers. The series combines energetic action with lively character interactions and darker questions about responsibility, loss and protecting others. It is an engaging choice for viewers who enjoy supernatural battle systems, ensemble casts and a more unsettling edge to their coming-of-age stories.
